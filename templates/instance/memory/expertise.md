# Expertise and competency

> The end state: what the AI can do without being told, and how that is measured.
> Replaced by evidence, not by opinion.

## Levels

| Level | Meaning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| reflex | done without being asked | the rule is in `AGENTS.md` or a check |
| enforced | a tool or script refuses the wrong path | the check fails when broken |
| handbook | written down but still needs a reminder | a knowledge entry, `Seen` ≥ 2 |
